How to Install the App

The app is added to your iPhone's home screen from the Safari browser. You do not need to download it from the App Store.

ℹ️ One-time setup only
You only need to do this once. After that, just tap the icon on your home screen to launch the app.
1
Open Safari
Open Safari (the compass icon) on your iPhone.
(Not Chrome or Firefox)
2
Enter the URL
Type the following in the address bar at the top:
steakteppei.github.io/order-app
3
Tap the Share button
Tap the square with an arrow pointing up (Share button) at the bottom of the screen.
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll up on the menu that appears from the bottom, then tap "Add to Home Screen".
5
Tap "Add"
Tap "Add" in the top right corner. The icon will be added to your home screen.
The page opened in Safari
Select "Add to Home Screen"
The icon is added to your home screen
⚠️ Important
From now on, always launch the app by tapping the Steak Teppei icon on your home screen.
Do not open it from Safari.

Chapter 2

Ordering Process (Overview)

Every order follows the same 4 steps. Once you get the hang of it, it takes less than 5 minutes.

The app's home screen
1
Select a supplier
Tap the supplier button at the top of the screen. The selected button turns orange.
2
Select a delivery date
Tap a date on the calendar. Grayed-out dates cannot be selected.
3
Enter quantities
Press "+" to enter the quantity. Items left at 0 are not included in the order.
4
Send the order
Tap the orange "Preview & Send" button, review the contents, and send.

Q&A
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

If you run into any issues, check here first.

Q
The app won't open / the screen is blank
A
First, fully close the app and reopen it.
1. Double-tap the Home button (or swipe up from the bottom of the screen)
2. Swipe the Steak Teppei app up to close it
3. Tap the icon on your home screen to reopen

If it still doesn't work, check if you're connected to Wi-Fi.
Q
The "Preview & Send" button is grayed out and can't be tapped
A
Make sure all three of the following are done:
✅ Selected a supplier (button is orange)
✅ Selected a delivery date (tapped on the calendar)
✅ Entered at least one quantity (not all zeros)

Once all three are done, the button turns orange and becomes tappable.
Q
The date I want is grayed out and can't be selected
A
Grayed-out dates are either non-delivery days for that supplier or past dates.

If you need a delivery on a holiday or special occasion, tap "Holiday / Special Schedule" and turn the switch ON. This will make all dates selectable.
However, you should confirm with the supplier first. A "special schedule" note will be automatically included in the order message.
Q
I sent an order by mistake
A
There is no cancel function in the app.
Open Gmail, LINE, or Messages directly and contact the supplier to make corrections.
The sooner you reach out, the better.
Q
After tapping send, Gmail doesn't open and nothing happens
A
Tap the "Copy Order Message" button to copy the order contents.
Then open the Gmail app manually, create a new email, enter the recipient, paste the text, and send.

The To and CC addresses are displayed on the app screen.
Q
My Costco order doesn't reach $1,000
A
Below the product list, you'll see "$XX more to reach $1,000 min" showing how much more you need.
Increase quantities of other items you need until you exceed $1,000.
Once you pass $1,000, a green "✓ Min. order met" message appears.
Q
The app seems stuck on an old version
A
Try fully restarting the app:
1. Double-tap the Home button and swipe the Steak Teppei app up to close it
2. Make sure your iPhone is connected to Wi-Fi
3. Tap the icon on your home screen to reopen the app

This will automatically update the app to the latest version.
Q
The weather shows "Weather unavailable"
A
This appears when you're not connected to the internet.
Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data, then reopen the app.
Q
What does the red "NOW" badge mean?
A
It indicates a busy or slow period that is currently in progress.
For example, if "Japan Golden Week" shows a "NOW" badge, it means there are currently more Japanese tourists than usual.
Use this to adjust your order quantities accordingly.
Q
What does the "👥↑" icon mean?
A
This icon appears on days when rain or storms are forecasted.
Based on past data, rainy days tend to bring more customers.
"👥↑" means "expect more customers" and "👥↑↑" means "expect significantly more."
Consider ordering a bit extra the day before.
Q
I forgot to add the app to my home screen. Can I do it again?
A
Yes, you can do it as many times as you need.
Open Safari, go to steakteppei.github.io/order-app, and follow the steps in Chapter 1 again.
Q
Can I add items that are not on the product list?
A
Yes. Tap "+ Add custom item" below the product list and type in any product name.
The added item will be included in the order message.
