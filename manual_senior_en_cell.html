Ordering Process (Overview)

Every order follows the same 4 steps. Takes less than 5 minutes once you're used to it.

The app's home screen
1
Select a supplier

Send the order

Q&A
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

Q
The app won't open / screen is blank
▼
A
Fully close the app and reopen it.
1. Double-tap Home (or swipe up from bottom)
2. Swipe the app up to close
3. Tap the icon to reopen

Still not working? Check your Wi-Fi connection.
Q
"Preview & Send" is grayed out
▼
A
Make sure all three are done:
✅ Supplier selected (orange button)
✅ Date selected (tapped on calendar)
✅ At least one quantity entered (not all zeros)

All done? The button turns orange.
Q
The date I want is grayed out
▼
A
Gray = non-delivery day or past date.

Need a holiday delivery? Tap "Holiday / Special Schedule" and turn the switch ON. All dates become selectable.
Confirm with the supplier first — a special schedule note is auto-added to the message.
Q
I sent an order by mistake
▼
A
No cancel function in the app.
Open Gmail, LINE, or Messages and contact the supplier directly. The sooner, the better.
Q
Gmail doesn't open after tapping send
▼
A
Tap "Copy Order Message" to copy the text.
Open Gmail manually, create a new email, paste, and send.
To/CC addresses are shown on the app screen.
Q
Costco order doesn't reach $1,000
▼
A
You'll see "$XX more to reach $1,000 min" below the list.
Add more items until you exceed $1,000.
A green "✓ Min. order met" appears when you're there.
Q
App seems stuck on old version
▼
A
1. Double-tap Home, swipe app up to close
2. Check Wi-Fi is connected
3. Reopen from the home screen icon

This forces an update to the latest version.
Q
"Weather unavailable" message
▼
A
No internet connection. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and reopen.
Q
What does the red "NOW" badge mean?
▼
A
A busy or slow period is happening right now.
e.g., "Japan Golden Week" with "NOW" = more Japanese tourists right now.
Adjust order quantities accordingly.
Q
What does "👥↑" mean?
▼
A
Rain/storms are forecasted. Past data shows rainy days bring more customers.
"👥↑" = more customers expected. "👥↑↑" = significantly more.
Order extra the day before.
Q
Can I redo the home screen setup?
▼
A
Yes, anytime. Open Safari, go to steakteppei.github.io/order-app, and follow Chapter 1 again.
Q
Can I add items not on the list?
▼
A
Yes. Tap "+ Add custom item" below the product list and type any product name. It'll be included in the order message.
